Connect your store

Choose one: install the plugin for your shop, or upload a spreadsheet of your orders.

1. Get your store key

The key lets your shop send orders to Athena. Keep it private, like a password.

Demo mode: store registration isn't built yet, so this key is made up and won't work for a real shop.
You won't see this key again. Save it somewhere safe now.

2. Install the plugin

Shopify, WooCommerce and Square plugins are coming soon. Until then, your developer can send orders straight to Athena:

curl -X POST https://<athena-address>/api/transactions \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -H "X-API-Key: YOUR_STORE_KEY" \
  -d '{"order_id": "A-1042", "transaction": {"TransactionAmt": 49.99, "P_emaildomain": "gmail.com", "DeviceType": "mobile"}}'

Or: upload a CSV of orders

Export orders from Excel or your sales system as CSV. Columns Athena understands: order_id, amount, email (or email domain), device (desktop / mobile), card_type. The dataset's own column names (TransactionAmt, P_emaildomain…) also work.

Demo mode: the bulk-upload endpoint isn't built yet, so each row is sent as a single order (up to 500 rows).
